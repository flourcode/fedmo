Background
Mark Flournoy
Founder of Fedmo

For most of my career, I've helped technology companies understand how government buys, who influences decisions, where partners fit, and how to turn good technology into real federal revenue.

I led the Federal Partner Vertical Team at AWS, where my team was responsible for partner-originated revenue across DoD, civilian, financial, and national security markets. As an individual contributor, I closed a $54M four-year committed cloud contract with a $7B DoD systems integrator. Before AWS, I was an early federal seller at F5 and Red Hat through significant growth phases at each. I've also worked the government side — as a Contracting Officer's Technical Representative at DISA — which means I understand how buying decisions actually get made, not just how vendors think they do.

I spent 20 years as a Marine officer, including time at USSOCOM supporting global special operations networks and on General Mattis' staff in Iraq. I have an M.S. in IT Management from the Naval Postgraduate School and a geology degree from Auburn. Along the way I've been a Marine, a government program leader, a federal seller, and a startup builder. That combination shows up in how I think about problems.

I founded Fedmo, a federal market intelligence platform used by federal sellers and BD teams to map agency spending, partner ecosystems, and expiring-contract pipelines using live USASpending and SEC EDGAR data.

Advisory work is taken on selectively — by introduction, referral, or direct inquiry. If you've found this page, reach out with a sentence or two about your situation. The right fit matters more than the number of engagements.
