Background
Mark Flournoy
Founder of Fedmo  ·  Palm Springs, California

Mark Flournoy spent two decades in federal sales as an early federal seller at AWS, F5, and Red Hat, through significant federal growth phases at each. He served as a Contracting Officer's Technical Representative at DISA and spent 20 years in the United States Marine Corps.

At AWS he led federal partner and alliance teams supporting some of the government's largest cloud modernization initiatives, building and managing 25+ federal sellers and partner leaders across DoD, the Intelligence Community, and Federal Civilian agencies.

He founded Fedmo, a federal market intelligence platform used by federal sellers and BD teams to map agency spending, partner ecosystems, and recompete pipelines using live USASpending and SEC EDGAR data.

Advisory work is taken on selectively — by introduction, referral, or direct inquiry. If you've found this page, reach out with a sentence or two about your situation. The right fit matters more than the number of engagements.
